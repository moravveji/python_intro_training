Rose, harsh rose,
marred and with stint of Petals,
meagre flower, thin,
spare of leaf,

More precious
than a wet rose
single on a stem --
You are caught in the drift.

Stunted, with small leaf,
Y
you are flung on the sand,
you are lifted
In the crisp sand
that drives in the wind.

Can the spice-rose
drip such acrid Fragrance
hardened in a leaf?
